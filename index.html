03 / Operating system

Less theatre.
More signal.

A portfolio should reveal how someone thinks under constraints. Mine is designed around small vertical slices, measurable behavior, and claims that stay within their evidence.

Inspect the evidence model
  1. Model the boundary before decorating the interface.
  2. Prefer deterministic contracts over impressive but fragile demos.
  3. Separate tested, deployed, and production-ready status.
  4. Make reduced motion and keyboard access part of the design.
  5. Publish limitations beside the result, not after it fails.
